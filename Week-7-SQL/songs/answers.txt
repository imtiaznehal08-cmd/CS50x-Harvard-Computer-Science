1. Audio Aura Characterization:
The listener's top 100 songs from 2018 have an average energy of 0.659,
valence of 0.484, and danceability of 0.716. This suggests an aura that is
moderately energetic and highly danceable, but with a somewhat neutral or
bittersweet emotional tone. The listener tends to gravitate toward music
that gets you moving, but not necessarily music that feels purely happy.

2. Limitations and Better Approaches:
Averaging all 100 songs equally does not reflect how often each song was
actually played. A song streamed 500 times should carry more weight than
one streamed 5 times. A weighted average by play count would be more
representative.

Additionally, the mean can be skewed by outliers — a few extremely high-energy
or low-energy songs can shift the average away from what the listener
actually prefers most. Using the median, or focusing only on the top 10
most-played songs, might give a more accurate picture of the listener's
true audio aura.
